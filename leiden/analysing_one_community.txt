Largest Community Analysis – Conclusions

To better understand the low correspondence between the Leiden communities
and political parties (NMI = 0.15), we examined the largest detected
community in more detail.

Community 0 contains 76 MPs across 10 party labels. VVD is the largest
party represented, with 23 MPs (30.3%). Therefore, no single party
dominates the community.

Within Community 0, 579 edges connect MPs from different parties.
These are cross-party MP-pair edges: each edge represents a pair of MPs
from different parties who collaborated on at least one motion. The value
579 therefore represents edges, not the total number of motions.

The most frequent cross-party connections by number of MP-pair edges are:
- CDA – VVD: 63
- D66 – VVD: 54
- CDA – D66: 39
- PvdA – VVD: 35
- CDA – SP: 31

Overall, the analysis shows that the largest Leiden community is not
equivalent to a single political party. MPs from several party labels
appear together in the same collaboration community and are connected
through cross-party collaborations. This provides a concrete example of
the limited correspondence between the detected communities and the
formal party partition indicated by the overall NMI of 0.15.

Something to be careful of:
The number of edges between two parties should not directly be interpreted
as the strength of collaboration between those parties. Parties with more
MPs have more possible MP pairs. This analysis is therefore used as a
descriptive case study of the largest detected community.
